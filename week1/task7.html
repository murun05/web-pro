How to Make Kimchi Fried Rice

What You Need

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1/2 cup chopped kimchi
  • 1 tablespoon kimchi juice
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 fried egg

Steps

  1. Heat sesame oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the chopped kimchi and sauté for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the cooked rice and stir vigorously to combine everything evenly.
  4. Pour in the kimchi juice and cook until the rice absorbs the liquid.
  5. Top with a fried egg and serve immediately.
